Features

Everything you need, one tab away

Six simple tools that work together, so the plan for your day is waiting every time you open a new tab.

Projects & tasks

Group tasks into projects like Home, Kids or Work. Use a list or a board, set priorities and dates, and move things along as you go.

Calendar

See your day, week or month. Scheduled tasks sync with Google Calendar, next to the calendars you already use.

Bookmarks

Organise links into spaces and collections. Save all your open tabs in one click and find anything with search.

Notes

Notebooks with pages for lists, plans and recipes. Write long documents or pin quick sticky notes, and link them to tasks.

Home dashboard

Your most visited sites and favourite shortcuts, shown as icons, cards or a list, plus a panel of your open tabs.

Sync across computers

Your workspace is backed up to a private folder in your own Google Drive and merged across every computer you use.

Calendar

Your tasks and your calendar, side by side

Give a task a time and it appears on your calendar, in its own "Tabono Tasks" calendar in Google. Drag it to move it, stretch it to change how long it takes.

  • Day, week and month views
  • See your other Google calendars alongside
  • Repeating tasks: daily, weekly, monthly and more

Privacy

Private by design

TABONO has no servers. Your information stays on your computer and in your own Google account.

Your data stays yours

Tasks, notes and bookmarks are saved in your browser and backed up to your Google Drive. We never receive a copy.

Nothing is sold

No ads, no analytics and no tracking. We don't collect anything about you or how you use TABONO.

Browsing stays private

Your most visited sites and open tabs are read on your device only, to show them on your new tab. They are never sent anywhere.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Is TABONO free?

Yes. TABONO is free to install and use, with no ads.

Where is my information stored?

In your browser on your computer, and in a private, hidden folder in your own Google Drive. TABONO has no servers, so AlphaMindX never receives a copy.

Can I use TABONO on more than one computer?

Yes. Sign in with the same Google account on each computer and your workspace is kept in step through your Google Drive backup.

Why does TABONO need a Google account?

Signing in lets TABONO back up your workspace to your Google Drive and show your scheduled tasks in Google Calendar. It only changes events for its own tasks; your other calendars are shown read-only.

How do I delete my data?

Delete items in TABONO, or reset the whole workspace in Settings. You can also remove TABONO's backup from Google Drive and revoke its access in your Google account. The privacy policy explains each step.
