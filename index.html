Everything you need, one tab away

Six simple tools that work together, so the plan for your day is waiting every time you open a new tab.

Projects & tasks

Group tasks into projects like Home, Kids or Work. Use a list or a board, set priorities and dates, and move things along as you go.

Calendar

See your day, week or month. Scheduled tasks sync with Google Calendar, next to the calendars you already use.

Bookmarks

Organise links into spaces and collections. Save all your open tabs in one click and find anything with search.

Notes

Notebooks with pages for lists, plans and recipes. Write long documents or pin quick sticky notes, and link them to tasks.

Home dashboard

Your most visited sites and favourite shortcuts, shown as icons, cards or a list, plus a panel of your open tabs.

Sync across computers

Your workspace is backed up to a private folder in your own Google Drive and merged across every computer you use.

Private by design

TABONO has no servers. Your information stays on your computer and in your own Google account.

Your data stays yours

Tasks, notes and bookmarks are saved in your browser and backed up to your Google Drive. We never receive a copy.

Nothing is sold

No ads, no analytics and no tracking. We don't collect anything about you or how you use TABONO.

Browsing stays private

Your most visited sites and open tabs are read on your device only, to show them on your new tab. They are never sent anywhere.

Up and running in a minute

  1. Add TABONO to Chrome

    Install it from the Chrome Web Store and open a new tab.

  2. Sign in with Google

    If you've used TABONO before, your workspace is restored from your Google Drive.

  3. Plan your day

    Create a project, add tasks and see them on your calendar.
