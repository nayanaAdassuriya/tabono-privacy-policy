TABONO is a Chrome productivity workspace for organizing projects, tasks, schedules, bookmarks, and notes. You sign in with Google; your workspace is stored in your browser, your tasks sync with your Google Calendar, and backups go to a private folder in your own Google Drive. TABONO has no servers and never receives your data.

This site provides information about TABONO and its data practices.
